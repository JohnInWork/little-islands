## Why this pack is here

Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup has no street lamps — it has no streets. The town was
lighting itself with campfires, which is what Ivan saw and objected to. These
are lamp posts: a light on a pole, standing on the ground.

## What is here

The upstream `PNG/` tree, minus the GIMP sources:

- `posts/` — 32×32-tiled posts, three tiles tall. Two lamp heads (`lamp-1`,
  `lamp-2`) on two post styles (`post-1` plain, `post-2` with a finial), each
  `tall` or `short`, with one arm (`single`) or two (`twin-T`, `twin-U`), in
  three colours (grey by default, `-blue`, `-bronze`).
- `hanging/` — 16×32 wall-hung versions of both lamps, same three colours.

These files are **not** covered by the CC0 dedication that applies to the
surrounding Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup library.
