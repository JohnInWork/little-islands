## What is here

Four tiles, cut unmodified from the pack's `floors.png` at (128, 1248): the
honey-coloured brick-laid plank floor, as a 2×2 block that repeats seamlessly.

The pack holds over a hundred interior floors and the game draws one of them.
Only the four tiles it actually asks for are shipped, the same way the rest of
the sprite library is kept: the itch packager copies what the catalog names.

Of the four wooden patterns in this shade, this is the only one whose block
tiles without banding — the others alternate boards running across and along,
so repeating them lays visible stripes across a room.

These files are **not** covered by the CC0 dedication that applies to the
surrounding Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup library, and they are a different pack from
`licensed/lpc-tavern` (CC-BY-SA 3.0) even though both come from LPC.
